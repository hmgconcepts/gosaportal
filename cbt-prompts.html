AI Question Prompts (for CBT CSV)

🧩 What is this page?

Writing exam questions by hand is slow. This page gives you ready-made prompts you can paste into any free AI chat (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, etc.) to instantly draft questions in the exact CSV format the CBT page accepts.

The platform itself uses no paid AI — you simply copy a prompt, fill in the blanks ([TOPIC], [NUMBER], [CLASS/LEVEL]), paste it into a free chat, copy the CSV it returns, edit it to suit your needs, save as a .csv file, and upload it on the CBT page. Pick the level that matches your need:

🎛 Prompt Studio — build a tailored prompt in seconds

Fill the boxes once — the studio writes the whole prompt for you, correctly formatted for the pack you choose. Includes the advanced 17+ question types, multi-subject papers with per-subject topics, 📚 Digital Library reading packs (link-based) and 📝 Assignment briefs (link-based).

Structured like HMG Academy CBT Pro: strict CSV header, type distribution, deterministic scoring, no paid API.

✅ Step-by-step

  1. Click Copy prompt on the level you want.
  2. Open any free AI chat in a new tab and paste the prompt.
  3. Replace [TOPIC], [NUMBER] and [CLASS/LEVEL] with your details, then send.
  4. Copy the CSV the AI returns. Review and edit every question for accuracy (you are the teacher!).
  5. Paste it into a spreadsheet or a plain text file and save as questions.csv.
  6. Go to the CBT page → create an exam → Upload CSV. Done.
