🖨️ Fee collection statement — fully paid vs outstanding (PDF)

A printable statement for management, the PTA or an audit. It lists every student who has paid in full and every student who has not, and shows the individual instalments that make up each student's payments. Opens the browser's print dialog — choose Save as PDF to keep a copy.

🛠️ One-click fee repair

Use this after you change an old receipt (add a forgotten debt, correct an amount, fix a wrong term/date), or whenever a balance looks wrong. It rebuilds every receipt snapshot, recomputes arrears and outstanding for every student, re-syncs the portal-lock state, and reports anything it cannot fix safely — duplicates, unlinked receipts, overpayments and missing fee structures.

📄 Invoices — record what each learner was billed

A bill only exists once it has been raised. Before V12.67 this page guessed a learner's past bills from the school calendar, which is how a parent who owed nothing came to be shown previous terms owing on the dashboard. Invoices are now a real record: raise them once per term, and the dashboard, this page, the receipts and the parent portal all read that one figure.

🧾 Fee Truth Sheet — one student, every number explained

Every screen in School Connect now shows the same five numbers for a student: total fees this term, paid, balance this term, arrears, and total outstanding. If a parent or the bursar ever questions a figure, open this sheet: it prints the arithmetic line by line, names why the bill is what it is, lists every receipt and says which ones count for this term. Print it and hand it over.

🩺 Fee Doctor — find every error before a parent does

Fourteen checks covering everything that can make a balance wrong: receipts with no term, receipts attached to no student, zero or negative amounts, double entries, learners paying against no bill, learners with no fee structure at all (a zero bill that is not a zero debt), overpayments, opening balances, future-dated receipts, CBT scores counted in two report columns, aid that names nobody, undated academic periods, and conflicting class fee structures. Each finding names the student, says what it means, and tells you what to do. Fixable problems are repaired; money records are never deleted by a tool.

🔎 Bill coverage audit — where does every “Total fees this term” come from? (V12.68)

One question, answered for the whole school at once: for the current term, how many learners are billed from the exact term price list, from the “Current Term” label, from another term of the same session, from the latest structure, from an invoice — and how many have no bill at all. A learner with no bill is reported as unknown, never as ₦0, and the audit names the reason and the page that fixes it. This is the check that proves the Round-9 “₦0 total fees” fault is gone for every learner, not just the two reported.

📑 Master Financial Records — export by class (V10.1)

One row per student: current-term bill, scholarship/aid, paid so far, balance, previous-term arrears and TOTAL outstanding — computed live by the same engine that powers the dashboards. Print/save as PDF or download CSV. Needs database/complete-schema.sql.

💰 Daily Fee Collection Dashboard

Know exactly what entered the school each day. Select a date to see total payments, transaction count, average receipt, payment methods, classes, collectors and an auditable transaction list.
