🎯 The mission

“Recurring payments should not keep your schools from having online presences.” Many capable schools stay offline because every vendor demands monthly, termly or yearly fees. HMG platforms are offered for a single, low one-time setup fee — after that the school pays ₦0 to run it, forever. Subscription plans exist only for schools that prefer to spread payment, and even then an expired subscription never deletes data: it simply waits, alive, until renewal.

🧠 The engineering philosophy

Free tools only — Supabase, Vercel/Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Actions. No paid AI APIs — every intelligent feature (report comments, CBT grading, timetable engine, bots) is deterministic and rules-based, so it can never surprise the school with a bill. Privacy by construction — PostgreSQL Row-Level Security guards every table. Clients own everything — code, database and hosting live in accounts the school controls.
